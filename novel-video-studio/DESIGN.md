# Novel Video Studio Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
An editorial night studio for Thai storytellers: deep ink surfaces, warm parchment panels, restrained plum highlights, and precise production controls. It should feel like a quiet recording room rather than a marketing landing page. The manuscript is always the hero.

## Colors
- Ink canvas: `#0e1018`
- Raised panel: `#171a26`
- Parchment: `#f4efe6`
- Plum accent: `#a780ff`
- Voice teal: `#66d6c0`
- Warning coral: `#ff7d6e`

## Typography
- UI and Thai copy: IBM Plex Sans Thai
- Story/display voice: Noto Serif Thai
- Metadata and time: IBM Plex Mono

## Motion
- Calm 180-260ms fades and short vertical movement
- Progress animation only where work is active
- No bouncing controls or decorative infinite motion

## What NOT to Do
- No oversized landing-page hero
- No purple gradients across the whole screen
- No nested cards
- No glassmorphism that reduces text contrast
- No tiny video captions
